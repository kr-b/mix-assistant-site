Setup

Three steps, about five minutes.

You need Live 12 Suite, or Standard with Max for Live, version 12.3 or later on macOS, and an OpenRouter account with a few dollars of credit for the explanations. Measuring works without one.

Step 1

Put the folder in your User Library

Unzip the download. Drag the Mix Assistant folder onto User Library in Live's browser, or copy it into Music/Ableton/User Library. Keep the files inside it together.

User LibraryMix AssistantDevices
Step 2

Drop MixAssistant on the Master

Make it the last device on the Master track. Then choose where the track is going: Streaming, Club, or Master when a mastering engineer takes it from here (then it checks headroom instead of loudness). That's remembered for this project.

Step 3

Add your OpenRouter key

Press Chat. Paste a key from openrouter.ai/keys. It's checked for free before it's saved. Set a daily and monthly limit; the defaults are $1 a day and $10 a month.

Everyday use

Play, analyze, listen, decide.

The everyday loop takes about a minute. Nothing in your set changes until you press Apply, and Revert takes it out again.

Song & arrangement

Does the drop actually lift?

Arrange reads your locators and clips without playing anything. Song also measures the song as it plays in Live: loudness, low end and brightness, section by section. Name your locators (Intro, Build, Drop…) for the best results.

Arrange No playing needed

Reads where clips sit and how sections are named. It points out drops that don't add anything, sections that aren't whole phrases, long stretches where nothing changes, a slow start for streaming, and little drums-only room for DJs.

Song Measured as it plays

Press Song, then play from bar 1. It stops by itself a bar after the last clip. Loops and jumping around are fine: each bar is measured where it played. The temporary recording is deleted right after. Each review is remembered, so the next one starts with what changed.

Where devices go

One device on the Master. Listeners where you want detail.

A MixListener lets the assistant hear a track on its own, so it can tell that the kick and bass fight, or that a pad buries the vocal, and suggest the fix on the track that causes it. Turn the options on to see where each device belongs.

The meter

Reading the verdict.

The target is for a whole song, so the dot compares the integrated loudness of this play once 10 seconds have played. Five seconds of silence starts a new play. Drag the slider to see how the device judges it.

Green: within 1 LU of the target.
Amber: louder than the target. For streaming, the service will turn it down.
Red: far over. For streaming, above -9 LUFS; for club, more than 3 LU over.
Blue: quieter than the target.
Keep the Master fader at 0 dB. Like every device on the Master, Mix Assistant measures before the fader. If it isn't at 0 dB, the device shows a ! instead of a verdict. Control the level with devices before Mix Assistant, so what you measure is what you export.
The peak at the bottom right turns red above -1 dB. Keep true peaks under -1 dBTP so players and streaming encoders don't distort them.

Your audio never leaves your computer.

Your key is stored only in your Application Support folder, readable only by your user account. Heads-ups send nothing unless you press Explain.

What is sent to the AI

  • The measurements: numbers, not audio
  • Set, track and device names; for Arrange, locator names and where clips sit
  • For reference tracks, their names and measurements
  • Earlier advice for this project, and what you type
Help

When something looks off.

For anything not listed, click Save diagnostics in the chat window. It saves versions, settings, the last measurements and the recent log, with no audio and no key, and shows the file in Finder.

"No audio in the last 30 s"

The device only hears what plays through the Master while it's on. Play the track, then press Analyze. If it says audio isn't running, turn on audio in Live's settings.

The numbers are 6 dB away from my export

Check the Master fader. Everything is measured before it; keep it at 0 dB and set the level with devices before Mix Assistant.

I use a limiter on the Master

Put a MixListener on the Master before the limiter, and keep MixAssistant last. Analyze still measures loudness and peaks at the end of the chain, but tests fixes on the mix before the limiter, plays that in Before/After, and Apply puts the fixes in front of the limiter.

A reference track won't load

Only WAV files work; export or convert MP3 and AAC first. If the chat window inside Live doesn't open a file dialog, open the chat in your browser from ☰ in the device and add references there.

A MixListener says "Starting…" or isn't heard

Make sure it's the last device on the track and the track isn't frozen. Return tracks aren't supported yet.

The chat window is blank

Close it and press Chat again, or choose Open chat in browser under ☰ in the device.
